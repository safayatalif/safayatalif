I'm **Safayat Hossen Alif**, a **Lead Software Engineer** with **3+ years** of full-stack experience building and maintaining web portals, databases, and enterprise systems. At **Gigabit** I lead the delivery of production platforms for an Australian solar & energy group (**McKercher Corporation, Perth Solar Warehouse, PSW Energy**) — owning everything from requirement analysis and database design to secure REST APIs, real-time features, and cloud deployment with **NestJS, Next.js, PostgreSQL, Redis, BullMQ & Socket.IO**.

Previously at **Nextgen Innovation Ltd.**, I developed and deployed the **H360 Hospital Information Management System (ERP)** with **Laravel & Oracle** across multiple hospitals. My goal is simple: build efficient, user-friendly, and impactful digital solutions.
